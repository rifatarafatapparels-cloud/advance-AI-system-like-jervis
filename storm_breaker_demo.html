Governance checkpoints

✓ Apply applicable privacy and information-handling policy.
✓ Keep decisions with authorized human officials.
✓ Document provenance, limitations, and dissenting views.
✓ Separate exercise observations from verified facts.

System boundaries

National Security Command · presenter view

Briefing narrative

A senior-level introduction to the Storm Breaker coordination concept.

CONCEPT ONLYThis presentation makes no claim of actual AI, intelligence, or live command capability.

01 · Shared understanding

A common view of exercise assumptions

The interface illustrates one way to organize synthetic scenario context and discussion prompts across several fictional workstreams.

02 · Coordination, not automation

Human accountability at every stage

Its purpose is to support a structured tabletop conversation. It does not assess threats, prioritize operational actions, or issue recommendations.

03 · Safety by design

Transparent limits and governance

The demo is offline, uses only invented data, avoids personal and geographic details, and clearly labels all displayed content as synthetic.

04 · Questions for leadership

A responsible path for further exploration

◇ Which coordination problems merit a governed pilot?
◇ What authorities and safeguards must apply?
◇ How will success, uncertainty, and accountability be evaluated?
